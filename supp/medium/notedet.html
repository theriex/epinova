#serial: A Serial is a series of articles joined by an overview. When published separately, each article should start with a sentence noting it is part of a series, followed by a sentence stating what this particular discussion is going to be about. That second sentence somewhat echoes the title, but reaffirms the reader has come to the right place to hear about it.
#beyondcat: Even if standard categories have failed to provide the selection power needed for a personal music interface, personal music library retrieval is still fundamentally a data encoding and retrieval problem. It can be solved in software.
#musicovertime: My earlier collected music is an anchor to my younger self and what the music meant to me then. My perception of what the world was like as shared with the artist, and what I was like then. This is more than a snapshot in time I revisit for nostalgia, it's a connection to who I am, and where I am coming from. That part of me lives when I listen, and helps me where I am going.
